Lasagna

Description

To make this classic lasagna, start by layering a rich, savory meat sauce and melted cheese between broad pasta sheets in a baking dish. Bake it in the oven until the cheese is bubbly, golden brown, and completely melted. Let it rest briefly after baking so the slices hold their shape, then serve warm and enjoy!

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef or Italian sausage
  • Marinara or tomato sauce
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Grated Parmesan cheese
  • Garlic and Italian seasoning

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 190°C (375°F).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions and drain.
  3. Brown the meat in a pan, add your tomato sauce, and let it simmer.
  4. In a bowl, mix the ricotta cheese with seasonings.
  5. Layer the sauce, noodles, ricotta mixture, and mozzarella in a baking dish.
  6. Bake in the oven for 25 to 30 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
  7. Let it cool for 10 minutes before slicing and serving.
