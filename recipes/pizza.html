Pizza

Description

Crafting the ultimate homemade pizza starts with a resilient, well-kneaded dough that yields a golden, bubbly crust with just the right amount of char. Layered generously over the stretched base is a vibrant, herb-infused tomato sauce made from crushed San Marzano tomatoes, garlic, and a pinch of salt to balance the natural acidity. This is blanketed with torn fresh mozzarella cheese that melts into gooey, bubbling pools during a high-temperature bake. Finished off with a drizzle of olive oil and a scattering of fresh basil leaves right as it emerges from the oven, every slice delivers a harmonious blend of crisp texture, savory richness, and bright, aromatic flavor.

Ingredients

  • Pizza Dough
  • Crushed Tomatoes
  • Fresh Garlic
  • Fresh Mozzarella Cheese
  • Extra Virgin olive Oil
  • Fresh Basil Leaves
  • Kosher Salt

Steps

  1. Preheat the Oven: Turn your oven up as high as it will go (usually around 450°F to 550°F, or 230°C to 290°C). If you are using a pizza stone or steel, put it in the oven now so it gets scorching hot.
  2. Prepare the Dough: Stretch or roll out your pizza dough on a lightly floured surface or parchment paper until it reaches your desired thickness and shape
  3. Make the Sauce: In a bowl, mix your crushed San Marzano tomatoes with minced fresh garlic and a pinch of salt.
  4. Assemble the Pizza: Spread a thin, even layer of the tomato sauce over the stretched dough, leaving a small border around the edges for the crust.
  5. Add the Cheese: Distribute the torn fresh mozzarella evenly across the sauce.
  6. Bake: Transfer the pizza to your preheated oven or pizza stone. Bake for 10 to 15 minutes, or until the crust is golden-brown and the cheese is bubbling and slightly charred.
  7. Garnish and Serve: Remove from the oven, top with fresh basil leaves, and drizzle lightly with extra virgin olive oil before slicing and serving.
